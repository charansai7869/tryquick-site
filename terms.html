5. Preview Fees and Payments

Preview fees are calculated by TryQuick based on the listed product's market price:

  • Physical preview fee: from ₹68, scaling with product price (e.g. ₹108 for a ₹10,000 product, ₹192 for a ₹60,000 product)
  • Digital preview fee: ₹29 – ₹69 depending on product price

All payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorized payment gateway. Fees are held safely after payment. Once the buyer shares the first 4 digits of their preview code (after seeing the product), the preview counts as delivered and the fee is non-refundable. Payments are made by UPI only — with a UPI app or a UPI ID.

Revenue split: 80% of the preview fee goes to the owner; 20% is retained by TryQuick as a platform fee. The Service Provider reserves the right to modify pricing at any time; any changes will be clearly communicated.

Preview fees are payment for the preview service itself — not for any product purchase. TryQuick does not sell goods.

6. Booking and Cancellation

When you request a preview, you submit three preferred time slots. If the owner confirms a slot, a booking is created and payment is required within the deadline shown in the app.

Cancellation rules:

  • Before payment is made: No penalty. Full refund if any amount was deducted.
  • After payment, before the journey starts, buyer cancels: ₹10 flat fee deducted at any stage once paid. Remainder refunded to wallet within 24 hours.
  • During an active physical preview, buyer cancels: Buyer forfeits 20% of the preview fee as a penalty; the remaining 80% is refunded to the buyer's wallet.
  • During an active physical preview, owner cancels: Buyer receives a full refund. The owner is charged a penalty of 15% of the preview fee, capped at ₹30, deducted from their future earnings.
  • Digital previews: free before payment; once paid, a flat ₹10 penalty applies to whoever cancels — including after the session starts.
  • Owner no-show (after the session has started): The buyer is refunded the preview fee minus 20% of the fee (₹10 for a video-call preview) — the same as cancelling after the session started. Contact details are shared the moment the session starts — that is the service TryQuick has rendered, and it is retained regardless of what happens afterwards. The owner receives no payment and is charged a penalty of 15% of the fee, capped at ₹30 (₹10 for a video-call preview). An owner cancellation before the session starts is different and is refunded in full.
  • Buyer no-show (after the session has started, preview code never shared): The buyer is refunded the preview fee minus a no-show penalty of 20% of the fee (₹10 for a video-call preview). The owner receives no payment and no penalty.
All cancellation penalties are retained by TryQuick as a platform charge. A penalty is never paid to the other party — cancelling does not earn the other person money, and is not a way to profit from a cancelled booking.

Repeated cancellations (2+ in a single day) may result in a temporary booking freeze.

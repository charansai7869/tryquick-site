4. User Roles

Buyers browse nearby product listings and pay preview fees to examine products from owners in person or via video call.

Owners list their products with photos, condition details, and pricing. They agree to meet buyers at scheduled times for preview sessions.

The same account can switch between buyer and owner roles within the app.

5. Preview Fees and Payments

Preview fees are calculated by TryQuick based on the listed product's market price:

  • Physical preview fee: ₹83 – ₹353 depending on product price
  • Digital preview fee: ₹29 – ₹69 depending on product price

All payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorized payment gateway. Fees are held in escrow and released upon successful preview completion.

Revenue split: 80% of the preview fee goes to the owner; 20% is retained by TryQuick as a platform fee. The Service Provider reserves the right to modify pricing at any time; any changes will be clearly communicated.

Preview fees are payment for the preview service itself — not for any product purchase. TryQuick does not sell goods.

6. Booking and Cancellation

When you request a preview, you submit three preferred time slots. If the owner confirms a slot, a booking is created and payment is required within the deadline shown in the app.

Cancellation rules:

  • Before payment is made: No penalty. Full refund if any amount was deducted.
  • After scheduling, buyer cancels: ₹10 flat fee deducted. Remainder refunded to wallet within 24 hours.
  • During active preview, buyer cancels: Buyer forfeits 20% of the preview fee. Owner receives 15% (up to ₹30) as compensation. Remaining 65% refunded to buyer.
  • Owner cancels or no-show: Buyer receives a full refund. Owner may receive a penalty.

Repeated cancellations (2+ in a single day) may result in a temporary booking freeze.

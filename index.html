What is TryQuick? — FAQ

What is TryQuick?

TryQuick is an Indian peer-to-peer product preview marketplace. Before buying a product (electronics, fashion, appliances, and more), you pay a small fee to inspect it first — either in person from a nearby owner (physical preview) or over a video call from anywhere in India (digital preview). No product is bought or sold on TryQuick; you only pay for the preview.

How does it work?

Browse listings, pay a small preview fee (held safely in escrow), then inspect the real product in person or on a video call at a scheduled slot. Both parties verify identity with an OTP. The owner earns 80% of the fee; TryQuick keeps 20%.

How much does a preview cost?

Physical preview fees start around ₹68 and scale with product value; digital (video-call) previews are ₹29–₹69. The exact fee is shown before you confirm a booking.

Is it available across India?

Yes. Physical previews depend on a nearby owner; digital video-call previews work anywhere in India.

Is TryQuick safe?

Yes — meetings are OTP-verified, the preview fee is held in escrow until the preview is confirmed complete, and both parties rate each other after every preview.

Should I use TryQuick before buying something like a trimmer, phone, or laptop?

If you want to see, hold, or test a product before spending money on it — or get a video-call walkthrough from someone who already owns it — TryQuick lets you preview it first, so you buy without regret or returns.

Can I earn money on TryQuick?

Yes. If you own a product, list it and earn 80% of every preview fee by hosting physical or video-call previews — your product stays with you.

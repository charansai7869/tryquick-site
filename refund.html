Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 2, 2026

What Are Preview Fees?

Preview fees are charged by TryQuick when a buyer books a time slot to physically or digitally examine a product from an owner. This fee covers the owner's time and the platform service — it is not a purchase of goods.

No goods are bought or sold through TryQuick. The preview fee is purely for the inspection service.

Cancellation Rules & Refund Amounts

How Refunds Are Processed

  • Refunds are credited to your TryQuick in-app wallet within 24 hours of the cancellation or dispute resolution
  • Wallet balance can be withdrawn to your bank account from the Wallet screen within 3–5 business days
  • Original payment method refunds (direct to card/UPI) are not available — all refunds go to TryQuick wallet first
  • Platform fee (20%) is non-refundable once a preview session is completed successfully

Repeated Cancellations

Cancelling 2 or more bookings in a single day will trigger a temporary booking freeze on your account. This is to protect owners' time and ensure platform reliability.

Owners with unpaid penalties of ₹30 or more cannot create new listings until the penalty is cleared from their wallet.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Preview was completed successfully and buyer simply changed their purchase decision
  • Buyer was not satisfied with the product condition but the listing accurately described it
  • Buyer did not show up and did not cancel (counted as buyer no-show)
  • Account was terminated due to policy violations or fraud

How to Cancel a Booking

Open the TryQuick app → My Bookings → tap the booking → Cancel Booking. The cancellation penalty (if any) will be shown before you confirm.

Cancellations cannot be made after a preview session has been marked as active (journey started). In that case, raise a Dispute instead.

How to Raise a Dispute

If something goes wrong during a preview (owner no-show, product mismatch, safety concern), raise a dispute within the app: My Bookings → tap the booking → Raise Dispute.

Our team reviews disputes within 24 hours. Escrow funds are held until the dispute is resolved. The decision of TryQuick's support team is final for amounts up to the preview fee.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 4, 2026

What Are Preview Fees?

Preview fees are charged by TryQuick when a buyer books a time slot to physically or digitally examine a product from an owner. This fee covers the owner's time and the platform service — it is not a purchase of goods.

No goods are bought or sold through TryQuick. The preview fee is purely for the inspection service.

How Refunds Are Processed

  • Refunds are credited to your TryQuick in-app wallet within 24 hours of the cancellation or dispute resolution
  • Wallet balance can be withdrawn to your UPI ID from the Wallet screen, usually within 24 hours (the UPI ID you paid with is saved automatically; you can change it)
  • Refunds go to your TryQuick wallet. If you paid directly through UPI at checkout, a refund may instead go back to that UPI account
  • Platform fee (20%) is non-refundable once a preview session is completed successfully

Where Penalties Go

Every cancellation penalty shown above is retained by TryQuick as a platform charge. A penalty is never paid to the other party.

This is deliberate: if cancelling earned the other person money, it would create an incentive to provoke cancellations. Penalties exist to discourage last-minute cancellations, not to compensate anyone.

Repeated Cancellations

For Owners: Cancelling 2 or more bookings in a single day will trigger a 1-day account suspension. During suspension, the owner cannot accept new requests or create listings.

For Owners: Owners with unpaid penalties of ₹30 or more cannot create new listings until the penalty balance is cleared from their earnings.

For Buyers: Cancelling 2 or more bookings in a single day pauses new booking requests for 24 hours.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Preview was completed successfully and buyer simply changed their purchase decision
  • Buyer was not satisfied with the product condition but the listing accurately described it
  • Buyer did not show up — the no-show penalty above applies (20% in person / ₹10 video call)
  • The first 4 digits of the preview code were shared — the preview counts as delivered
  • Account was terminated due to policy violations or fraud

How to Cancel a Booking

Open the TryQuick app → My Bookings → tap the booking → Cancel Booking. The cancellation penalty (if any) will be shown before you confirm.

You can still cancel after the journey has started — the penalties in the tables above apply.

How to Raise a Dispute

If the other person doesn't show up or the owner shows a different product, raise a dispute within the app: My Bookings → tap the booking → Something went wrong? The app only offers the options that apply at that stage. For a safety concern, use Report User — it's handled separately from payments.

Disputes are resolved within 24 hours by the fixed rules above, based on the preview-code record from your session — the decision is final for amounts up to the preview fee.

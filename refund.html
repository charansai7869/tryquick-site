Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 2, 2026

What Are Preview Fees?

Preview fees are charged by TryQuick when a buyer books a time slot to physically or digitally examine a product from an owner. This fee covers the owner's time and the platform service — it is not a purchase of goods.

No goods are bought or sold through TryQuick. The preview fee is purely for the inspection service.

Buyer Cancellation Rules

When Buyer Cancels Buyer Refund Owner Impact
Before owner picks a time slot (pending / slots submitted) Nothing charged — no payment taken yet No impact
After slot confirmed by owner, before buyer pays Nothing charged — no payment taken yet No impact
After payment, before journey starts (scheduled status) Preview fee minus ₹10 flat cancellation charge No penalty on owner
After physical preview journey starts (active / escrow locked) 80% refunded — 20% of fee is non-refundable (preview already started) No earnings — booking cancelled
After digital preview session starts (active / escrow locked) Preview fee minus ₹10 flat fee (contact details were shared) No earnings — booking cancelled
Booking expires (2-hour payment deadline missed) Nothing charged — no payment was taken No penalty

Owner Cancellation Rules

When Owner Cancels Buyer Refund Owner Penalty
Owner declines request before picking a slot Nothing charged to buyer No penalty
Owner cancels after confirming a slot (before journey starts) 100% refund to buyer wallet ₹10 penalty added to owner's dues — deducted from next payout
Owner cancels or no-shows during physical preview 100% refund to buyer wallet 15% of preview fee, capped at ₹30 — deducted from future earnings
Owner cancels during digital preview session 100% refund to buyer wallet ₹10 flat penalty — deducted from future earnings

Dispute Outcomes

Outcome Buyer Refund Owner Impact
Dispute resolved in buyer's favour Up to 100% at TryQuick's discretion Penalty may apply
Dispute resolved in owner's favour Partial or no refund, at TryQuick's discretion Owner receives payout

How Refunds Are Processed

  • Refunds are credited to your TryQuick in-app wallet within 24 hours of the cancellation or dispute resolution
  • Wallet balance can be withdrawn to your bank account from the Wallet screen within 3–5 business days
  • Original payment method refunds (direct to card/UPI) are not available — all refunds go to TryQuick wallet first
  • Platform fee (20%) is non-refundable once a preview session is completed successfully

Repeated Cancellations

For Owners: Cancelling 2 or more bookings in a single day will trigger a 1-day account suspension. During suspension, the owner cannot accept new requests or create listings.

For Owners: Owners with unpaid penalties of ₹30 or more cannot create new listings until the penalty balance is cleared from their earnings.

For Buyers: Frequent cancellations are monitored and may result in temporary account restrictions to protect owners' time.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Preview was completed successfully and buyer simply changed their purchase decision
  • Buyer was not satisfied with the product condition but the listing accurately described it
  • Buyer did not show up and did not cancel (counted as buyer no-show)
  • Account was terminated due to policy violations or fraud

How to Cancel a Booking

Open the TryQuick app → My Bookings → tap the booking → Cancel Booking. The cancellation penalty (if any) will be shown before you confirm.

Cancellations cannot be made after a preview session has been marked as active (journey started). In that case, raise a Dispute instead.

How to Raise a Dispute

If something goes wrong during a preview (owner no-show, product mismatch, safety concern), raise a dispute within the app: My Bookings → tap the booking → Raise Dispute.

Our team reviews disputes within 24 hours. Escrow funds are held until the dispute is resolved. The decision of TryQuick's support team is final for amounts up to the preview fee.

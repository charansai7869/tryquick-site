Safety Guidelines

For Buyers — Physical Previews

📍
Meet in public placesCoffee shops, malls, or building lobbies. Never go to a stranger's home alone for a first meeting.
👥
Bring a friend if possibleEspecially for high-value items. There is safety in numbers.
🕐
Meet during daytimeSchedule previews during daylight hours in well-lit, populated areas.
📱
Tell someone where you're goingShare the meeting location and owner's name with a family member or friend before you go.
✅
Check the owner's verification badgeVerified owners have completed KYC. Prefer verified owners for high-value products.
🚫
Don't pay outside the appAny request to pay cash, UPI, or outside TryQuick is a scam. All legitimate transactions go through the app wallet.

For Owners — Hosting Previews

📍
Choose a safe meeting spotYour home entrance lobby, a café, or a community area. You are not required to invite buyers inside your home.
🔒
Keep valuables securedOnly bring the specific product being previewed. Leave other valuables at home.
📷
Take photos before handing overDocument the product's condition before letting a buyer handle it, in case of a dispute.
🚫
Never accept off-app paymentsIf a buyer tries to pay you directly or asks to "settle outside TryQuick," report them immediately.

For Digital Previews (Video Call)

✅
Owners must be KYC-verifiedDigital previews are only offered by identity-verified owners. This protects buyers who cannot meet in person.
🎥
Record the call (with consent)Both parties may record the video preview for their own reference in case of disputes.
🔍
Ask to see all sidesRequest the owner show all angles, ports, screen, and any damage during the call. A good owner won't refuse.
🚫
Don't share personal informationNever share your home address, bank details, or Aadhaar number during a video preview.

Red Flags — Report These Immediately

  • Someone asks you to pay outside the app (cash, UPI, NEFT)
  • An owner or buyer asks for your bank account or OTP
  • A listing that looks too good to be true (brand-new iPhone for ₹500 preview fee)
  • Threats, harassment, or pressure to make decisions quickly
  • Meeting location changed last minute to somewhere isolated
  • Owner refuses to show the product clearly or makes excuses during a digital preview

If something feels wrong, leave immediately and report it in the app.

TryQuick's Safety Measures

  • OTP-verified phone numbers for all accounts — no anonymous users
  • KYC identity verification for all digital preview owners (Aadhaar + selfie)
  • Escrow-held payments — owners receive money only after preview completion
  • In-app dispute system with 24-hour resolution
  • Account suspension for policy violations, no-shows, and fraud
  • Rating system so the community self-regulates bad actors

🚨 Safety Concern or Emergency?

Email support@tryquick.in immediately

For life-threatening emergencies, call 112 (India emergency services) first.

Privacy Policy

Who We Are

TryQuick is a peer-to-peer product preview marketplace operated by Charan Saiyesh Reddy Medapati ("Service Provider", "we", "us"), based in Thane, Maharashtra, India.

TryQuick connects buyers who want to physically or digitally examine a product with nearby owners who list their products for short preview sessions. Buyers pay a small preview fee — no goods are sold through the platform itself.

By using TryQuick, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app and website.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Mobile phone number (used for OTP-based login via MSG91)
  • Full name and age
  • Profile photograph
  • Home location (for matching buyers with nearby owners)
  • Aadhaar card photo and selfie (KYC — for owners offering digital previews only, required by RBI regulations)
  • Product listing details: photos, condition, description, pricing
  • Booking preferences and slot selections
  • Ratings and review text

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • IP address
  • App usage patterns and session timestamps
  • Push notification token (Firebase FCM)
  • Precise location (when permission granted — core feature for nearby matching)

Payment information:

Preview fees are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorized payment gateway. We do not store full card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs. Razorpay handles all payment data under their own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your identity via OTP login
  • Match buyers with nearby product owners based on location
  • Facilitate bookings, slot scheduling, and preview session coordination
  • Process preview fee payments and manage escrow via Razorpay
  • Verify owner identity for digital preview eligibility (KYC — RBI compliance)
  • Send booking confirmations, payment alerts, and time-sensitive notifications via Firebase FCM
  • Calculate preview fees based on product price
  • Manage wallet credits, refunds, and owner payouts
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations
  • Improve app performance and user experience

Location Data

TryQuick uses your device's location to show nearby product listings and match you with owners in your area. Location is a core feature of the service — the app cannot function without it.

We use precise location only while the app is active. We do not track your location in the background.

Owner home locations are stored on our servers for the distance-based matching feature. Exact addresses are never shown to buyers — only approximate distance and city name are displayed.

KYC / Identity Verification

Owners who offer digital preview sessions must upload an Aadhaar card photo and a selfie. This requirement exists because:

  • RBI regulations require identity verification for peer-to-peer financial payouts
  • Buyers cannot physically verify an owner's identity during a video call — verified identity builds essential trust

KYC documents are stored securely on Cloudflare R2 (encrypted object storage). Access is restricted to our review team only. Documents are not shared with third parties except as required by law.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Between users: When a booking is confirmed, limited contact details (name, phone number) are shared between buyer and owner to facilitate the preview meeting
  • Payment processors: Razorpay receives transaction data to process payments and payouts
  • Legal requirements: If required by Indian law, court order, or a regulatory body (RBI, law enforcement)
  • Fraud prevention: Data may be shared with agencies to detect or prevent fraud or abuse

Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while active and for 90 days after deletion
  • Booking records: 3 years (financial compliance)
  • KYC documents: 5 years (RBI regulatory requirement)
  • Payment transaction records: 7 years (Indian accounting law)

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data by emailing support@tryquick.in
  • Correction: Update your profile information within the app at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your account via Settings → Delete Account. This anonymizes your personal data. Financial and KYC records are retained for legal compliance.
  • Notifications: Disable push notifications in your device settings or within the app
  • Location: Revoke location permission in device settings at any time (note: core features require it)

Children's Privacy

TryQuick is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately at support@tryquick.in and we will delete the account promptly.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS / TLS for all data in transit
  • Encrypted object storage for KYC documents (Cloudflare R2)
  • JWT-based authentication with secure token storage on device
  • Role-based database access controls
  • Signed URLs for all media (24-hour expiry)

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to support@tryquick.in before public disclosure.

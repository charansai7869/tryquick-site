Who We Are

TryQuick is a peer-to-peer product preview marketplace founded and operated by Charan Saiyesh Reddy Medapati ("Service Provider", "we", "us"), based in Thane, Maharashtra, India.

TryQuick connects people looking for product previews ("buyers") with people who own those products ("owners"). A buyer on TryQuick is not someone purchasing a product — they are someone seeking a preview session. An owner is not a seller — they are someone who hosts the preview of a product they own. No goods are sold through TryQuick.

For physical previews, buyers are matched with owners nearby based on location. For digital previews (video call), matching is not location-restricted — any owner across India can host a digital preview.

By using TryQuick, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app and website.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Mobile phone number (used for OTP-based login via MSG91)
  • Full name and age
  • Profile photograph
  • Home location (for matching buyers with nearby owners)
  • Aadhaar card photo and selfie (KYC — for owners offering digital previews only, required by RBI regulations)
  • Product listing details: photos, condition, description, pricing
  • Booking preferences and slot selections
  • Ratings and review text

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • IP address
  • App usage patterns and session timestamps
  • Push notification token (Firebase FCM)
  • Precise location (when permission granted — core feature for nearby matching)
  • Pages visited, time and date of visits, time spent on pages

Payment information:

Preview fees are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorized payment gateway. We do not store full card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs. Razorpay handles all payment data under their own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your identity via OTP login
  • Match buyers with nearby owners for physical previews; match by product availability for digital (video call) previews regardless of location
  • Facilitate bookings, slot scheduling, and preview session coordination
  • Process preview fee payments and manage escrow via Razorpay
  • Verify owner identity for digital preview eligibility (KYC — RBI compliance)
  • Send booking confirmations, payment alerts, and time-sensitive notifications via Firebase FCM
  • Calculate preview fees based on product price
  • Manage wallet credits, refunds, and owner payouts
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations
  • Analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve app performance
  • Contact you with important information, required notices, and service updates

Location Data

TryQuick uses your device's location to show nearby product listings and match you with owners in your area. Location is a core feature of the service — the app cannot function without it.

We use precise location only while the app is active. We do not track your location in the background.

Owner home locations are stored on our servers for the distance-based matching feature. Exact addresses are never shown to buyers — only approximate distance and city name are displayed.

Aggregated, anonymized location data may be used to analyze user behavior, identify trends, and improve the app's performance and functionality. Anonymized location data may periodically be transmitted to external analytics services.

Identity Verification (Optional)

Owners may voluntarily submit an Aadhaar card photo and a selfie to receive a Verified badge on their profile. This is entirely optional — it is not required to list products or offer digital previews. Completing verification simply signals additional trustworthiness to preview seekers.

Submitted documents are stored securely on Cloudflare R2 (encrypted object storage). Access is restricted to our review team only. Documents are not shared with third parties except as required by law.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website (tryquick.in) does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It is a static informational site and sets no analytics or marketing cookies, so no cookie-consent banner is required to browse it.

The TryQuick mobile app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (listed above) to understand feature usage and diagnose crashes. These use device identifiers rather than browser cookies. You can limit this by adjusting your device's advertising/analytics settings, and analytics collection is disabled in development builds.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Between users: When a booking is confirmed, limited contact details (name, phone number) are shared between buyer and owner to facilitate the preview meeting
  • Payment processors: Razorpay receives transaction data to process payments and payouts
  • Legal requirements: As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process, or if required by Indian law, court order, or a regulatory body (RBI, law enforcement)
  • Safety and fraud prevention: When we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request
  • Trusted service providers: Third-party providers who work on our behalf, have no independent use of the information, and have agreed to adhere to the rules in this privacy statement

Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while active and for 90 days after deletion
  • Booking records: 3 years (financial compliance)
  • KYC documents: 5 years (RBI regulatory requirement)
  • Payment transaction records: 7 years (Indian accounting law)

To request deletion of your data, use Settings → Account Actions → Delete Account within the app. Financial and KYC records are retained for legal compliance regardless of deletion request.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the app marketplace.

  • Notifications: Disable push notifications in your device settings or within the app at any time
  • Location: Revoke location permission in device settings at any time (note: core features require it)
  • Account deletion: Delete your account via Settings → Account Actions → Delete Account within the app
  • Data access: Request a copy of your personal data via Settings → Account Actions → Request Data Copy — downloads a JSON file with all your account data, bookings, ratings, and wallet transactions directly to your device
  • Correction: Update your profile information within the app at any time

Children's Privacy

TryQuick is not intended for users under 13 years of age. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@tryquick.in and we will delete it from our servers promptly.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information and provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information we process and maintain:

  • HTTPS / TLS for all data in transit
  • Encrypted object storage for KYC documents (Cloudflare R2)
  • JWT-based authentication with secure token storage on device
  • Role-based database access controls
  • Signed URLs for all media (24-hour expiry)

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly through the Report a Bug option in Settings within the app before public disclosure.
